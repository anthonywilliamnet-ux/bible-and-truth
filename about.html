About the Author

Bible & Truth is written by Anthony William. The aim is simple: to help believers understand what the Bible teaches, to explain the historic Christian faith, and to answer hard questions, including questions raised by Islam, with accuracy and respect.

Every article tries to quote sources fairly and to point you back to Scripture so you can check it for yourself.
